# Relict: Field Archive – Abandoned, Not Forgotten

An interactive web archive that tells the stories of NASA hardware left on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space. Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (challenge: Abandoned but Not Forgotten).

**Live project:** https://arjith123.github.io/relict-field-archive/

## What it does
Students begin at Earth and open 12 field records covering 58 years of exploration, from Surveyor 1 in 1966 to Ingenuity in 2024. Each record shows what a machine was, why it was sent, how its mission ended and what science it left behind. A section on engineering shows how design choices made discovery possible, and a chart compares planned and actual mission lifetimes. A 10-question quiz at the end earns a Space Historian badge.

## How to run it
No installation is needed. Open the live link above, or open `index.html` in a web browser. An internet connection is needed to load NASA photos and fonts.

## Built with
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript (single-file project)
- Google Fonts: Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono

## NASA data and resources
- NASA Image and Video Library API: https://images-api.nasa.gov
- NASA Planetary Data System: https://pds.nasa.gov/
- NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/
- NASA mission pages on science.nasa.gov, linked in the Sources section of the site

All photos in the archive come from the NASA Image and Video Library. All mission facts come from NASA sources.
